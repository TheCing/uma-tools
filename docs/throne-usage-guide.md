## Recreation Timing

- The Tokai Teio Recreation raises Mood
- The 2nd Throne Recreation is best right before Senior-year Summer Camp

### The Tokai Teio Recreation Raises Mood

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1302385583.PNG)

Taking The Throne's Assemblage's Tokai Teio Recreation raises Mood. Since it's the Recreation that can restore Mood lost to consecutive-race penalties, be deliberate about when you take it.

It's most efficient to use it to restore lost Mood during race-dense stretches — the G1-heavy period before Summer Camp, or around October–December.

### The 2nd Throne Recreation Is Best Before Senior-Year Summer Camp

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1302516995.PNG)

The best timing for The Throne's Assemblage's final Recreation is right before the Senior-year Summer Camp. It puts you into the **Pure Passion Zone** — where Friendship Training triggers on every facility — setting up big training growth.

It also raises Mood, so even if bad events or consecutive racing have dragged your Mood down, you can head into camp with both Mood and Energy topped up.

#### The Pure Passion Zone Can Trigger at Other Times

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1305050253.PNG)

Besides the final Recreation, the Pure Passion Zone also triggers when Recreations first unlock, and randomly when training together.

Neither of those can be timed on purpose, so when you want a guaranteed window of big stat growth — i.e. before Summer Camp — use the final Throne Recreation to enter the Pure Passion Zone deliberately.

- Mile-to-Long (Turf) rotation

For Turf rotations running Mile through Long, plan around consecutive racing and take the Tokai Teio Recreation in early June or early January. Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi's Recreation has the largest Energy recovery, so using it the turn before a 2-race streak lets you dodge the penalty.

Finish the individual Recreations by early January; then the April/May Recreation plus the late-June one completes the chain, putting you in the Pure Passion Zone for Summer Camp.

## Recommended Support Decks

- Throne + Speed/Guts core
- Throne + Guts/Wit core
- Throne + Speed/Power core
- Throne + multiple Pal/Group cards

### Throne + Speed/Guts Core

| | | | | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example 1 | Speed | Guts | Guts | Guts | Guts | Group |
| Example 2 | Speed | Speed | Guts | Guts | Guts | Group |

A deck built around Speed and Guts supports with The Throne's Assemblage. Using Wit sparks † plus the Recreation stat gains, it grows every stat except Stamina evenly.

With no Wit support equipped, raising Wit is somewhat harder and the difficulty is on the higher side — but depending on your Speed supports, the range of learnable skills is wide, making it a very versatile build.

#### Use for Sprint and Mile Training

The Throne + Speed/Guts core grows every stat except Stamina to a high standard, making it an excellent build for Sprint and Mile training.

With a comparatively high count of Speed supports, it picks up strong rare skills easily, and is very commonly used for Champions Meeting training.

*Related: [Leo Cup 2022 training guide (Altema, JP)](https://altema.jp/umamusume/reohai)*

### Throne + Guts/Wit Core

| | | | | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example 1 | Guts | Guts | Guts | Guts | Wit | Group |
| Example 2 | Speed | Guts | Guts | Guts | Wit | Group |

A deck centered on Guts supports with a Wit card and The Throne's Assemblage, raising training consistency. Depending on which Guts supports you own, you can swap in a Speed support to pick up strong rare skills.

Training is stable, but watch out: Wit gains are modest, and depending on your Guts supports, stat growth and skill acquisition can be somewhat harder.

#### Use for Sprint and Mile Training

The Throne + Guts/Wit core also grows everything except Stamina to a high standard — excellent for Sprint and Mile training.

Guts training is easy to run consistently and average stats end up high, so this build shines when you're chasing evaluation score, or when you can't afford many training attempts.

### Throne + Speed/Power Core

| | | | | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example 1 | Speed | Speed | Power | Power | Wit | Group |
| Example 2 | Speed | Speed | Power | Power | Power | Group |

Adding The Throne's Assemblage to a Speed/Power-centered deck makes everything except Guts easy to grow. It slots strong Power supports naturally and works for Stamina-hungry Long-distance training.

Since Guts is hard to raise here, prioritize Climax-scenario sparks † or Guts sparks † in your inheritance and aim for a balanced stat line.

#### Use for Medium and Long Training

The Throne + Speed/Power core grows everything except Guts to a high standard — excellent for Medium and Long training.

Skills and stats depend heavily on which supports you own, but among Throne decks this one handles longer distances best, so it's recommended for Medium-distance Champions Meetings.

### Throne + Multiple Pal/Group Cards

| | | | | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example 1 | Guts | Guts | Guts | Wit | Group | Group |
| Example 2 | Speed | Speed | Stamina | Power | Pal | Group |

A deck pairing The Throne's Assemblage with other Recreation-capable Pal or Group supports. Completing all the chains takes many turns, but because the stats come from events, there's little downside variance from bad training rolls.

Combine it with supports that have high initial-stat bonuses, or with multiple supports of the same type, for very consistent runs.

#### Use for Team Trials Training

The Throne + multiple Pal/Group build is recommended for Team Trials training, where you race a lot and struggle to level your training facilities.

The Recreation stat gains and initial-stat bonuses lock in a solid baseline, making this build well suited to grinding out many runs.

## Cautions When Adding The Throne's Assemblage to a Deck

- Always train together as soon as the run starts
- Can't be used when training the characters listed on the card
- Can't be deck-mates with those characters' support cards

### Always Train Together as Soon as the Run Starts

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1302327336.PNG)

The Throne's Assemblage unlocks its Recreations via an event that triggers after training together. The trigger is random, so train together as early as possible to give it every chance to fire.

### Can't Be Used When Training the Characters Listed on the Card

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1302358654.PNG)

The Throne's Assemblage can't be equipped when training any character listed in its group info — including all alternate outfits of those characters. Keep this in mind when training Symboli Rudolf or Tokai Teio.

And although she isn't implemented yet, the same will presumably apply to Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi if she's ever added as a trainable uma.

### Can't Be Deck-Mates with Those Characters' Support Cards

The Throne's Assemblage also can't be placed in the same deck as Symboli Rudolf, Tokai Teio, or Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi support cards.

### Symboli Rudolf Recreation

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1302412696.PNG)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Effects | Energy +13 · Wit +36 · **Fighting Spirit** hint Lv +1 · Friendship Gauge +5 |

The Rudolf Recreation has low Energy recovery and no Mood gain, making it the easiest one to fit anywhere. Since its timing barely matters, use it to dodge consecutive-racing penalties.

### Tokai Teio Recreation

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1302449607.PNG)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Effects | Energy +13 · Mood +1 · Speed +24 · **Prudent Positioning** hint Lv +1 · Friendship Gauge +5 |

The Teio Recreation is the only one of the three individual Recreations that raises Mood. Use it to recover from a consecutive-racing penalty or an unlucky bad event.

### Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi Recreation

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_130247525.PNG)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Effects | Energy +39 · Skill Pt +18 · **Full Throttle** hint Lv +1 · Friendship Gauge +5 |

The Tsuyoshi Recreation restores the most Energy of all this card's Recreation events. It's ideal for avoiding races at 0 Energy — use it the turn before a racing streak.

### The Throne's Assemblage Recreation (1st)

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1318161421.jpg)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Effects | Energy +26 · Speed +12 · Wit +30 · Skill Pt +18 · **Nakayama Racecourse ○** hint Lv +1 · cures **Slacker** † · Friendship Gauge +5 |

The Throne group Recreations restore Energy and give large stat boosts. Like the Tsuyoshi Recreation, use it the turn before a racing streak to dodge penalties.

It has also been confirmed that this event cures the **Slacker** † bad condition. **Overweight** † and **Skin Outbreak** † were *not* cured during testing, so it appears to cure only certain bad conditions.

### The Throne's Assemblage Recreation (2nd)

![](https://img.altema.jp/umamusume/uploads/2022/08/2022y08m04d_1302516995.PNG)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Effects | Energy +26 · Mood +1 · Speed +18 · Wit +36 · Skill Pt +24 · **Refraction Arc** hint Lv +1 · enter **"Pure Passion Zone: The Throne's Assemblage"** · Friendship Gauge +5 |

The final Throne Recreation restores Energy, raises Mood, *and* enters the Pure Passion Zone. Every effect dovetails with training, so use it right before the Senior-year Summer Camp.

## Translator's Notes & Flags

Skill names are **confirmed Global names** (verified against our Global data files), matching the [Labo review translation](throne-guide.md). Items flagged **†**:

1. **Bad-condition names** — なまけ癖 → "Slacker", 太り気味 → "Overweight", 肌あれ → "Skin Outbreak". These are my best recollection of the Global condition names; verify against in-game strings.
2. **"Sparks"** (因子) — Global's term for inheritance factors; "Climax-scenario sparks" renders クライマックス因子 (the scenario factor from the Climax/Make a New Track!! scenario).
3. **Source garble** — one sentence in the Sprint-to-Mile section is corrupted in the original (叶せな, almost certainly 可能性); translated by intent and footnoted inline.
4. **Stat totals differ from the Labo article by design** — Altema's table counts only the five Recreation events (Speed +54, Wit +102, Skill Pt +60); the Labo table also includes the unlock/Classic-January/end-of-run events (Speed +96, Wit +186, Skill Pt +102). Both are correct for what they count.
5. The source title says 玉座**を**集いし者たち (a typo for 玉座**に**); the correct card name is used throughout.
