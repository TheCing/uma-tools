# Stamina & Survival Rate Guide

Understanding how stamina works is crucial for building competitive horses in Uma Musume.

## What is Survival Rate?

The **Survival Rate** shows how often your horse finishes a race without running out of stamina. When a horse "dies" (runs out of stamina), it dramatically slows down and usually loses the race.

- **100% Survival Rate** = Your horse never runs out of stamina
- **Low Survival Rate** = Your horse often runs out of stamina before finishing

## How Max Stamina is Calculated

Your horse's maximum stamina pool depends on three things:

1. **Your Stamina stat** - The primary factor
2. **Running Style** - Different styles use stamina differently
3. **Race Distance** - Longer races need more stamina

### Running Style Efficiency

| Running Style | Stamina Efficiency |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Betweener (Sasi) | 100% (Best) |
| Chaser (Oikomi) | 99.5% |
| Front Runner (Nige) | 95% |
| Stalker (Senkou) | 89% |
| Super Escape (Oonige) | 86% (Worst) |

This means a Betweener with 1000 Stamina has more effective HP than a Stalker with the same stat!

## What Drains Stamina?

Stamina drains based on how fast you're running above the "base speed" for the race. The faster you go, the more stamina you burn.

### Factors That Increase Stamina Drain

| Condition | Drain Multiplier |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Being Rushed (kakari) | 1.6x |
| Lead Competition | 1.4x |
| Lead Competition + Rushed | 3.6x |
| Oonige Lead Competition | 3.5x |
| Oonige Lead + Rushed | 7.7x (!!!) |
| Heavy Ground | 1.02x |
| Soft Ground | 1.01-1.02x |

### Factors That Reduce Stamina Drain

| Condition | Drain Multiplier |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Downhill | 0.4x (60% reduction!) |
| Pace Down | 0.6x |
| Higher Guts | Reduces drain in final phase |

## The Role of Guts

**Guts reduces stamina consumption during the final phase of the race** (middle and end sections). Higher guts = less stamina drain when it matters most.

The formula creates diminishing returns - going from 300 to 600 Guts helps more than going from 900 to 1200.

## Tips for Better Survival

1. **Match your stamina to the distance** - Longer races need more stamina
2. **Consider running style** - Betweeners are most stamina-efficient
3. **Avoid getting rushed** - Kakari state drains stamina fast
4. **Use stamina recovery skills** - Green skills that restore stamina can save a race
5. **Don't neglect Guts** - It helps conserve stamina in the crucial final stretch
6. **Check ground conditions** - Heavy/Soft ground increases drain slightly

## What Happens When Stamina Runs Out?

When your horse's stamina hits zero:
- Speed drops to minimum
- The horse starts decelerating rapidly
- Recovery is very difficult
- The race is usually lost

This is why maintaining a good survival rate is essential for consistent performance!
