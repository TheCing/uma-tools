Today's topic: **a performance review of the new SSR, The Throne's Assemblage**.

This support was added in the July 20 (Wed) banner update as a solo SSR pickup, and it is **the second Group-type support, following SSR Team \<Sirius\>**. Since Team \<Sirius\> was a welfare card, this makes it the first gacha Group support — and its strength is already the talk of the town, with more than a few people asking, "is this the strongest support card?!"

So **this article breaks down the performance of the much-discussed SSR The Throne's Assemblage**. I hope it helps you decide whether to pull on the support banner.

### Unique Bonus: Skill Point Bonus 2 at Friendship 80+

![](https://umamusumelabo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/4e376cc22ce1a4e4f3932a808e20ba7a-1024x427.jpg)

The Throne's Assemblage's unique bonus, **Reverence and Affection †**, grants **Skill Point Bonus 2 while the Friendship Gauge is 80 or higher**. Training alongside this support with the gauge at 80+ therefore pays out very nicely in Skill Points.

That said, **the unique bonus is more of a side dish** — **the real stars of this card are its standard bonuses and its event effects**. Let's look at those next.

### 35% Friendship Bonus Is Blazing Hot in the Pure Passion Zone

![](https://umamusumelabo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/result-1658502080-1024x776.jpg)

Here are the standard support bonuses, and the standout is unquestionably the **35% Friendship Bonus**. Group-type supports have the **Pure Passion Zone** — a window where every training counts as Friendship Training — so **a 35% Friendship Bonus during that window is enormous**. Considering SSR Team \<Sirius\> only had a 15% Friendship Bonus, the difference is night and day.

On top of that it carries **Speed Bonus 1** and **Training Effectiveness +5%**, making Friendship Training with it very strong. It also has a **10% Race Bonus**, which makes it comfortable to run in the Climax scenario — another point in its favor.

The table above sums **the stats gained if you complete every Recreation event** — as you can see, **that alone is +96 Speed, +186 Wit, and +102 Skill Points**. Add the gains from Friendship Training and the post-training bonus events on top, and the total climbs considerably higher.

These fixed event gains are so potent that **just having this support equipped makes Speed and Wit easy to stack**. Since Recreations are normally done in the gaps between races, you're padding stats while putting otherwise dead turns to work — extremely strong.

### Time Your Pure Passion Zone for Right Before Summer Camp

There are three ways to enter the **Pure Passion Zone**, the Group-type support's signature strength:

- **When Recreations unlock**
- **When you complete all Recreation events**
- **Randomly, when training together**

Of these, the only one you can time deliberately is **completing the Recreation chain**. **The standard play is to use it to enter the Pure Passion Zone in late June, right before Summer Camp.** Accordingly, enter the Takarazuka Kinen in whichever year (Classic or Senior) you are *not* using for the zone entry.

![](https://umamusumelabo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/e8570aa7f40106cdaf55046e3a8b5f30-1024x911.jpg)

Choosing between late June of Classic year and late June of Senior year:

- **By default, aim for late June of Senior year, when Friendship Gauges are filled.**
- **If you high-roll and gauges are already filled during Classic year, late June of Classic year is also an option.**

Personally, I recommend always planning around the Senior-year entry.

Also, **don't force yourself to chase Friendship Trainings during the very first Pure Passion Zone (the one from unlocking Recreations)**. You'll grow stats more efficiently by prioritizing raising your other supports' Friendship Gauges ahead of Summer Camp, so focus on those bonds first.

### Do Recreations Between Back-to-Back Race Stretches

![](https://umamusumelabo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/0feed6006047640d555414de378f0096-1-1024x538.jpg)

As for when to spend the Recreation turns: **just like with Pal supports, slot them into the gaps between consecutive races**. The idea is to recover from zero Energy via the Recreation, then launch into the next string of races on the following turns.

For the post-race choices, **pick the lower option — which has a chance of costing only −10 Energy — while making sure you won't hit 0**. The exception: if your Energy is in the 21–25 range, the lower option's fail pattern of −25 would be dangerous, so take the upper option's guaranteed −20 instead. You'll sometimes hit 0 Energy on the first race of a stretch; personally I just keep racing from 0 anyway.

Note that **only the Teio Recreation and the final Throne Recreation grant Mood +1**, so keep that in mind and put those boosts to use.

### Gold Skill "Refraction Arc" Is the Upgrade of Medium Corners ○

![](https://umamusumelabo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/b05ff965974ae04a25a8a3b542bda0da-1024x211.jpg)

**■ Activation condition**

When passing a randomly chosen corner (Medium distance only)

**■ Effect**

Velocity +3500 (white versions: 2500 (◎) / 1500 (○))

**■ Base duration**

3 seconds

---

The gold skill **Refraction Arc** is the upgraded gold of **Medium Corners ○**. Its Skill Point efficiency is worse than a ◎ corner skill, but **a reliably-activating gold with a 3-second base duration is simply strong**. It even fires inside the acceleration zone now and then — **for Medium distance runners, it's a strong skill you can pick up without hesitation**.

![](https://umamusumelabo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/f671f268083553cf62b685341ea6fae8-1024x211.jpg)

Being able to pick up **Prudent Positioning** from the Teio Recreation is also great. **It combos beautifully with Dodging Danger on Front Runners**, so it'll serve you well when training Front Runner umas.

## Verdict: One of the Strongest SSR Supports in the Current Meta

![](https://umamusumelabo.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/d932785484c2f13245676147e8ea20fa-1024x538.jpg)

So, the verdict on SSR The Throne's Assemblage: **"One of the strongest support cards in the current meta. Its stat-stacking power is clearly a cut above other supports — if you're short on Speed or Wit supports it's an obvious pull, and it's strongly recommended even if you're not."**

Frankly, this card is **so strong that it's close to a must-pull**. The usual schedule suggests a new training scenario is around the corner, and it may not be quite this dominant in the new scenario's meta — but it's a highly versatile card, so I don't expect a big drop-off. The SR El Condor Pasa picked up alongside it is also quite good, making this **a banner with very high pull value**. That said, the banner accompanying a new scenario is always guaranteed to be powerful, so **skipping this one to go all-in on the new-scenario banner is also perfectly valid**.

That's all for today. See you next time.
