Moomoolator is retired

November 2026

Thank you for racing with us

Moomoolator estimated skill and build value by running thousands of Monte Carlo approximations of a single uma on a course. It was always a model of the race rather than the race itself.

There are now race simulators that model the real thing, and they answer these questions better than this site ever could. So it's time for Moomoolator to step off the track.

Still here, as an archive

The main simulator stays online exactly as it was, but nothing is maintained any more. The other tools (skill visualizer, HP calculator, mechanics explorer, events, team trials, release timeline and the Canva guides) have been taken down. Game data is frozen as of November 2026, so new skills, umas and courses won't appear, and bugs won't be fixed.
